08FAQ

Straight answers

Do you invent reviews or photos?

No. Content comes from your public signals and what you confirm. Thin data means a thinner, honest site — not filler.

What does “claim” mean?

You get a live preview built for your business. When you claim, you complete the hand-over path (payment for paid packages, ownership instructions, one revision window). You’re not stuck in an open-ended project.

Why not a $200 freelancer?

Some freelancers are excellent. Many are cheap because scope, mobile quality, and hand-off are vague. Our packages fix those decisions up front. If you only need a temporary flyer page, DIY may be enough.

Why not a big agency?

Agencies shine on large brand systems, headcount, and long retainers. We ship productized, agency-grade assets with full ownership and clear packages — priced for value, not for office overhead. Multi-site operators who want a turnkey system without theater usually land in Lane 2.

I’m a weekend seller — do I need the $2,500 package?

Usually no. Lane 1 Identity ($500–$1,000) is for table and suite sellers who live on SMS, DMs, and Facebook — and for high-agency online anchors (personal or professional one-pagers) that need a clean front door, not multi-door ops. Lane 1 Shop ($2,500) is for 1–2 door shops with addresses, hours, and deeper trust needs.

We have many locations — or a real product platform — is that the $2,500 package?

No. Multi-site (several to many doors, location finders, operator-grade mobile paths) and product platforms (web, desktop, mobile, or all three) are Lane 2 — typically $10,000–$15,000 one-time turnkey (list often around $12k), full ownership, no retainer required. That is productized delivery under typical mid-market agency rebuild ranges for the same class of work. Haggling under that band usually means Lane 1 scope — or not a fit — not a soft L2 discount. Optional VAS (per-location SEO, deep modules, retainers) is quoted separately.

What if we’re not a fit?

We’ll say so. We’d rather decline than ship a site that pretends you’re a different business.
