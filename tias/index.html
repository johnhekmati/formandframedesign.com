    We hear you

    We love our customers — here are answers to the questions you ask most.

    Busy mornings, salsa preferences, mixed taco orders — if it’s come up at the window, we’ve got a clear answer so your next visit is easy.

    When is the drive-thru busiest?

    Morning taco runs and lunch rushes stack cars — that’s the hometown volume. If you’re on a clock, order ahead online, try curbside, or swing by a little off-peak.

    What if something’s off in the bag?

    Give the bag a quick look before you pull away, especially on mixed taco orders. If we missed something, call that location from the finder above — your hut can usually make it right faster than a delivery app.

    How do I get extra salsa (red or green)?

    Just ask at the window — “extra red” or “extra green” — and we’ll set you up. Confirm before you leave so you’ve got what you like with those tacos.

    Do all locations have the same hours?

    Most open early; some run later into the evening and a few close mid-afternoon. Use the location finder above for that door’s hours, phone, and directions.

    Can I order online?

    Yes — start at order.online, pick your location, and you’re set. Dine-in, drive-thru, and curbside are ready when you are.
